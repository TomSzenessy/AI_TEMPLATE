# Quality loop

This skill is the host invocation adapter. Read the canonical procedure and
oracles in [`../../../docs/verification.md`](../../../docs/verification.md), then apply
it to the current issue and declared surface.

Keep the skill invocation short:

1. Name the measurable quality bar and real-artifact oracle.
2. Give builders disjoint scopes and observable completion evidence.
3. Give a fresh critic the original acceptance criteria, diff, and artifact.
4. Fix confirmed blockers, rerun the oracle, and stop after three focused
   iterations; link unresolved disagreement to an issue.

Do not copy the procedure into this file; keep [`docs/verification.md`](../../../docs/verification.md)
as its single source of truth.
